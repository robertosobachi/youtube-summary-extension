Summaries

Stored only in this browser. Leave empty to keep the saved key.

Required to save a key. When you click Summarise, the video's title, channel and transcript are sent with your key to Anthropic's Claude API (api.anthropic.com), and billed to your Anthropic account.

Summary languages

A transcript in one of these is summarised in its own language, any other in English.

After a new summary, tells YouTube the video was played to the end, as its player does, so you can find it in your watch history. This also counts as a view. YouTube doesn't document these requests, so they may stop working without notice. Saved as soon as you tick or untick it.

Player

The speed button next to Summarise switches between normal speed and this one. Saved as soon as you pick it.

Feed

YouTube rounds dates, so "1 year ago" can mean up to 23 months. Applies to videos loaded after saving: reload YouTube for the ones already shown.
